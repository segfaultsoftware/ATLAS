# Personas

## Steve — Player (In-Session)

Steve is an active player using ATLAS at the table during a game session. He interacts with ATLAS as his in-game handheld device: crunching numbers for communications and logistics, interfacing with ship systems, tracking personal stats, viewing maps, and writing log entries in real time. Steve expects quick, responsive interactions that don't break the flow of play.

## Pam — Player (Prep/Between Sessions)

Pam is a player who engages with ATLAS between game sessions. She manages her character sheet, reviews rules in the SRD, reads up on ship systems, and plans for upcoming sessions. Pam values clear organization and easy navigation so she can find what she needs without digging through in-game interfaces.

## Gina — Game Master (GM)

Gina runs the game. She uses ATLAS as an orchestration tool — managing NPCs, encounters, world state, maps, and session logs. She references the SRD for rulings during play, and needs visibility into player-facing data like ship stats and crew rosters. Gina is always a separate person from the players.

## Nate — New Player / Learner

Nate is new to the ATLAS TTRPG system. He primarily uses the SRD to learn the rules, understand ship mechanics, and figure out character creation. Nate needs clear navigation, accessible language, and a low barrier to entry — he shouldn't need an account or an active campaign to start learning.

## Vera — Spectator / Non-Player

Vera isn't playing but is interested in the system. She might be browsing the SRD out of curiosity, evaluating whether to join a campaign, or following along with someone else's game. Vera has read-only access and doesn't need an account.
